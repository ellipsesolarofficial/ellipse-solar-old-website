How a Jaipur rooftop gets done

We walk you through this. Nothing moves to the next step until you have seen it.

  1. 01

    Free visit

    We come to the house or the site in Jaipur and read the bill with you.

  2. 02

    Rooftop survey

    We measure shadow-free area, check the structure, and note the meter and daytime load.

  3. 03

    Design

    A layout of the panels on your roof, with savings and payback, before you decide.

  4. 04

    Document corrections

    Subsidy, net metering, and Jaipur utility forms. If a paper is wrong, we correct it and file it.

  5. 05

    Loan help

    EMI papers, if you want them. Eligible homes are guided for ₹95,000 central subsidy and ₹17,000 state subsidy.

  6. 06

    Installation

    Mounting, panels, and inverter. A home rooftop is usually 2–3 days on site. Larger sites take longer.

  7. 07

    City handover

    Inspection and the new meter with the Jaipur utility. Then we show you generation and how the savings show up.
